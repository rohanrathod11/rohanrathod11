## now_building

Turning an investment idea into a strategy means defining the rules, testing
the assumptions, understanding the portfolio impact, and managing what
happens after deployment.

**TradePolaris** connects that process:

- **Vega** — turns questions into sourced research and helps develop testable strategies, with charts, underlying data, and cited sources.
- **Strategy testing** — evaluates explicit trading rules on unseen historical data through walk-forward backtesting, realistic costs, and overfitting checks.
- **Aegis** — the portfolio construction and risk engine. Combines constrained portfolio optimisation, factor-risk decomposition, VaR/CVaR, scenario stress testing, and ongoing risk monitoring to assess how a strategy changes the book and how the resulting portfolio behaves under stress.

**Broker-connected execution is the next layer:** carrying strategy rules
and portfolio constraints through to deployment.

The aim is to keep the evidence behind the idea, the rules behind the
strategy, and the controls around execution connected as the strategy
moves into use.
